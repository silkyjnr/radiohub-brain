### What we learned

1. **The video was the best ad**: highest CTR (6.1%), cheapest click ($0.73), most landing page views (70) from only 14% of spend. Meta gave most of the money to a weaker still. Next time, start with video and move budget to it fast.
2. **We couldn't see a single booking.** The Sales objective without a firing Purchase/Lead event is flying blind. Use Traffic (landing page views) until booking tracking is fixed.
3. **Generic "preserve your memories" copy (XMAS ad) had the worst CTR (~1.1%).** The early November creatives with specific copy got 5–6%.
4. **Wrong CTA:** the XMAS ad used `BOOK_TRAVEL`. Use Book Now / Learn More.
5. Frequency stayed under 2, so the ads didn't wear out. The problems were creative and tracking, not fatigue.
6. Two 2025 campaigns are still ACTIVE at campaign level with every ad paused (no spend). Tidy up.
