# Making Memories brand

Source: *Making Memories - Brand Guidelines.pdf* (Drive file `16EVfUbJOtlUTqK8OdRjAiooeuH3gPMAX`, in folder `1fuR3biAW7H1cFmaKL9Q5RJDjDvphAy4Z`). If the PDF changes, update this file.

## The product (say it right)

- **What:** a one-hour **guided** conversation in our Alexandria studio. A family member interviews a loved one (usually a parent or grandparent) from a **tailored question set**. We edit it into a **highlight video** delivered on a **private link**.
- **Price:** **$799**.
- **Tagline:** **"A guided conversation. A family story to keep."**
- **Buyer:** adult children and grandchildren, usually **as a gift**. Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day are peak times.
- **Gift option:** gift voucher (logo centred, recipient's name in gold script).
- Page: https://www.radiohub.com.au/makingmemoriesservices

These are flat colours sampled from the artwork. **Keep the metallic finish inside the logo.** Don't recolour it to flat gold.

## Logo

- Use the supplied **gold artwork**: `LOGO.png` (Drive `1nRPNAA0CFjPQQ_hpNMgV4Jw86BNBZw0o`, transparent PNG).
- Keep **every flourish** and the original proportions. No stretching, cropping, outlines or effects.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the "A" in MAKING on every side of the visible mark.

## Typography

| Font | Weight | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Great Vibes** | Regular | Titles, recipient names, short gold accents. Leave room for the swashes. |
| **Montserrat** | SemiBold 600 | Subheadings, quotes, captions, booking details, calls to action |

Both are free Google Fonts. Keep script text short (a few words). Never set body copy in Great Vibes.

## Layouts

**Campaign ad**
- **Navy wash over a photo collage** (real studio or family photos).
- New titles in **Great Vibes** (gold).
- **One action only**, e.g. "Book a session" or "Give the gift".

**Gift voucher**
- Logo centred above the recipient's name.
- Name in gold script (Great Vibes).
- Voucher number, date and booking details grouped beneath in Montserrat SemiBold.

**Portrait and quote (family stories in their words)**
- Keep the speaker's **face clear**. Don't put text over faces.
- Fade **Portrait Blue** behind the quote.
- Set their **exact words** in white Montserrat SemiBold. Don't paraphrase a real person's quote.

**Co-branding with RadioHub Media**
- **Slate Navy footer**. RadioHub Media logo on the **left**, Making Memories on the **right**.
- Keep the **pink inside the RadioHub logo** (don't recolour it to gold).

## Voice

- Warm, personal, unhurried. We're talking to someone about their mum, dad or grandparent.
- **Voice tip from the guidelines:** ask a question a family member would ask. *"Dad, how did you and Mum meet?"* *"Tell me about your first home."*
- Concrete beats abstract: "one hour in the studio", "a video you keep", "delivered on a private link", "$799".
- It's **guided**: reassure people they don't need to be good on camera. We do the questions, lighting, sound and edit.
- Avoid: morbid or guilt framing ("before it's too late"), clichés ("cherish forever", "priceless"), shouty caps, more than one emoji.
- Spell it **Making Memories** (title case) and **RadioHub Media** (one word, capital H).
