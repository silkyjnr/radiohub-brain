# RadioHub Media brand

Source: *RH Media - Brand Guidelines.pdf* (Drive file `1zVAHpKXBrhsVdkZOO3vxc9yrRw7reaTr`, folder `1boRF0wB0-UCChDklD0UdagQIvrzu0_eJ`).

**Line:** "Plan. Produce. Perform."
**Positioning:** a boutique Sydney production company. We help businesses plan, record and publish podcasts, videos and training content. radiohub.com.au

| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines on dark artwork, client quote cards |

White for small copy on dark layouts. On white backgrounds, keep pink for **large emphasis** and use navy or ink for reading text.

Script and condensed display styles from earlier campaigns are reference only. Don't use them in new work.

## Voice

- **Lead with what the client gets.** "One interview. Three clips for LinkedIn."
- Plain, confident, practical. We're producers, not hype merchants.

## Campaign layouts

- **Case study:** lead with the result. Show start figure, end figure and dates. **Recalculate percentage growth before publishing.**
- **Planning post:** one pink headline and one hero image (a halftone cutout works on ink). Keep the web address clear.
- **Educational post:** device mockup plus a short headline. Labelled cards for each point. Readable at feed size.
- **Service explainer:** show the work (e.g. device screens for transcripts/subtitles). Highlight one feature in pink.
- **Client testimonial:** client's words on a white panel, speaker or organisation named, paired with a real production still. **Keep the approved wording exactly.**
